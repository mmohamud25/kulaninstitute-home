Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

Overview

Nuur is a Chrome extension built by Kulan Group Ltd. This policy explains what data Nuur collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

What we collect

Nuur collects the following information to provide its features:

  • Profile information you voluntarily enter, including your name, email address, current role, and career goals. This is stored locally in your browser and used to personalize AI responses.
  • The URL and title of pages you visit while the extension is active. This is used to provide context aware assistance relevant to what you are currently viewing.
  • Text content from web pages you choose to analyze using Nuur features such as summarize, highlight, or research. This content is sent to the AI processing API and is not stored permanently.
  • Your interaction history with the Nuur chat interface, including messages you send and responses you receive. This is stored to maintain conversation context across sessions.

How we use your data

All data collected by Nuur is used solely to provide the features of the extension.

  • Your data is never sold to third parties.
  • Your data is never used for advertising purposes.
  • Your data is never used to determine creditworthiness or for any financial purpose unrelated to the extension.

Third party services

Nuur sends text content to Anthropic via a secure API to generate AI responses. Anthropic processes this content according to their own privacy policy available at anthropic.com/privacy. No other third party services receive your data.

Data storage

Conversation history and user preferences are stored in your browser using Chrome local storage. Profile data you choose to sync is stored in a private database accessible only to you.

Data retention

You can delete your conversation history at any time using the delete button on any chat in the sidebar. You can clear all stored preferences by uninstalling the extension.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any data stored by Nuur at any time. To request data deletion, contact us at the email below.
